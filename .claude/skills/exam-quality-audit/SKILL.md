# Workflow

1. Read course material explicitly selected for the assessment.
2. Verify answer indices, choice count, uniqueness, rationale, spelling and clarity in Arabic and technical English.
3. Distinguish recall, understanding and applied GIS tasks; flag questions with no supported correct answer.
4. Do not assume generated questions represent official instructor examinations.
5. Verify save-to-bank and review-loop behavior with unit/browser tests.
6. Present an audit matrix including source, correct choice, explanation and unresolved ambiguity.

Deliver: accepted, revised and rejected questions with evidence.
